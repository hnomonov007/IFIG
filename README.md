# International Financial Institution Group (IFIG)

International Financial Institution Group (IFIG) is a financial market intelligence and research project focused on global financial markets, macroeconomic research, market positioning, and analytical tools.

## About IFIG

IFIG provides a unified workspace for financial market research and analysis, covering areas such as:

- Forex
- Precious metals
- Commodities
- Stock market indices
- Equities
- Cryptocurrencies
- Macroeconomic research
- CFTC positioning
- Economic calendar
- Risk analysis
- Technical market analysis

The project is designed to help users monitor global markets, study macroeconomic conditions, review institutional positioning, analyze market catalysts, and conduct structured financial research.

Official website: https://ifig.vercel.app

## Founder & CEO

**NOMONOV HUSNIDDIN** is the Founder and CEO of International Financial Institution Group (IFIG).
