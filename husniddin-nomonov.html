About

NOMONOV HUSNIDDIN is the Founder and CEO of International Financial Institution Group (IFIG).

He is an Uzbek entrepreneur and financial-market researcher focused on global financial markets, Forex, macroeconomic analysis, trading, financial technology and market-intelligence systems.

He founded IFIG with the goal of developing a professional financial market intelligence and research ecosystem.
